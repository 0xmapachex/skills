# Design rules & review checklist

These are defaults that produce strong, conversion-focused social ads. Always defer to the brand's own guidelines where they conflict.

## Composition
- **Face is the #1 attention magnet.** Keep the hero's face visible, well-lit, and a clear focal point. Hide the *secondary* face (the customer) — shoot them from behind — never the hero.
- **Rule of thirds.** Put the face/subject near a thirds intersection. Put the text column on the opposing third. Avoid dead-center subjects.
- **Balance, not a centered island.** Subject on one side, text column on the other, so the two masses counterweight. A big empty area with nothing in it reads as a mistake.
- **Plan the text space INTO the photo.** You cannot reposition a subject after rendering. In the Codex prompt, explicitly request the subject offset (e.g. right-of-center) with clean, calm negative space on the opposite side for the text.
- **One alignment edge.** All text shares one margin.
- **Visual path.** logo → headline → subject's face → proof → offer/CTA. Check the eye travels cleanly.

## Type
- **Bold and big.** It must read at scroll speed on a phone. When unsure, go bigger/heavier.
- **Hierarchy:** small tag/eyebrow → large headline → subhead → loud offer.
- **One highlight device.** A single accent-color "tape" (accent background, dark text) behind ONE key word. Two highlights compete; keep it to one. Keep the highlight tight (a word, not a whole sentence).
- **Composite real fonts** so glyphs and accents (á, é, í, ñ, ¿, ¡) render perfectly. Add a subtle text-shadow/scrim for legibility over photos.

## Offer
- Make the offer (free trial, price) a **loud, distinct element** — not fine print. It is what converts.

## Platform formats & safe zones
| Format | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram/Facebook Story, Reels | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Keep text/logo within **top ~250px** and clear of the **bottom ~420px** — the app overlays the progress bar/handle on top and the native CTA + reply bar on the bottom. |
| Instagram/Facebook Feed | 1080×1350 (4:5) | More room; a drawn CTA is acceptable here. |
| Square | 1080×1080 | Generic feed/ad. |

## CTA
- **Stories/Reels: never draw a fake button.** It is not tappable, it competes with the platform's own CTA, and it sits in the bottom zone the UI covers. Instead: rely on the **native CTA** (set in the ads manager — e.g. "Send message" for click-to-message, "Learn more" for traffic) and add a small in-creative **cue** (short line + a down-chevron) pointing to it.
- **Static feed post (organic):** a drawn CTA/label is fine since there's no native button.

## Copy
- **Plain language for the actual audience.** No jargon, no abstract metaphors, no corporate-speak. Write how they talk; localize (dialect, currency, idiom).
- **Sell to the real buyer.** For tiny businesses the owner is buyer + user + payer + the person in pain — talk to that one person, by their trade, not to "businesses."
- **Lead with the pain, then the relief, then proof, then the offer.**
- **One idea per ad.** A blunt hook (a gut-punch question or flat claim) beats a clever line. Pick one verb.
- **Mind culturally loaded words.** A literal translation can carry class/role baggage (e.g. words that imply a servant). Check connotation in the target locale.
- Keep the brand wordmark/casing rules consistent.

## Anti-AI realism
- Real film grain, natural directional light, shallow depth of field, slight imperfection.
- Anatomically correct hands and faces; natural skin (no plastic gloss); no HDR/oversaturation.
- One coherent scene. No floating UI, no collage, no fake notification bubbles in the air (chat bubbles only on a real phone screen, and kept illegible).

## Final review checklist
- [ ] Hero face visible & prominent; secondary face hidden
- [ ] Thirds + balanced; planned negative space used (no dead void)
- [ ] Headline readable at scroll speed; clear hierarchy
- [ ] Exactly one highlight; good contrast
- [ ] Offer is loud
- [ ] Inside platform safe zones; no fake button in Stories (native CTA + cue)
- [ ] Accents/glyphs correct (composited type)
- [ ] Looks photographed, not AI-rendered
